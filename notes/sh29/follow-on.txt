sh30 draft: F2 the colour

Base the work on sh29's final merge, not the initial 1102894 checkout.
The owned display layer has buffer codepoints, cluster widths, pos_at,
move_to, draw_from and explicit EL-based shadow clearing. Shadow suffixes
are separate Ed.shadow values; only shadow_accept may insert them.
The pager owns rows below the prompt and restores the cursor on every draw.
Do not put colour escapes into Ed.buf, history, undo, or the kill ring.

First measure fish's default command/reserved-word/quote/variable/error
palette through a pty, separately for xterm-256color, linux and dumb.
Preserve SGR90 as the shadow default; indexed/RGB greys are optional
PELT_COLORS settings, not falsely described as fish defaults. Reset style
before ordinary input, cursor motion, pager rows and command execution.
The default-on friendly/suggest/histsearch switch waits on argv[0]'s re-pin;
exclude basename sh, -c, scripts, pipes and --posix. No !! or !$ work here:
F3 owns opt-in interactive histexpand; leading ! remains literal for F2.

Proposed implementation items:
1. A pure, nonexecuting line classifier; never expand/evaluate while drawing.
2. Command resolution and parser-state colours, including incomplete quotes.
3. One themed renderer shared by full redraw, incremental edit and resize.
4. PELT_COLORS names and 16/256/RGB selection with portable defaults.
5. Opt-in highlight, default policy coordinated with the argv[0] re-pin.
6. Typed colour/reset/resize/pager/shadow-interaction cases on both tiers.
7. Release per-key highlighting below 1ms for an 80-column line, no idle
   wakeups; planted wrong colour, leaked escape, and stale redraw rows.

PAX sessions remain pending until a px-owned harness slot exists. Cite
px70's actual palette/cell rows, not a claim of arbitrary RGB framebuffer
pixels. Do not build PAX or enter another lane's directory/CPU block.
