Fish measured as a program through an 80-column pty in the pinned Arch
container, kasumi CPUs 4-7, no implementation source read. Exact packages:
dash 0.5.13.4-1.1, bash 5.3.20-2, fish 4.9.3-1. Raw per-step bytes and final
commandline/cursor queries are in fish/*.json. Effects were separately
written by the out function; a final-only Ctrl-O binding queried the real
edit buffer, never the shadow. The fixture answers terminal DA and CPR.
Initial probes without the DA answer never reached a prompt and are not
measurements. --no-config omits the default colours; final probes use a
fresh HOME and normal initialization, without reading installed sources.

Exact-prefix history prefers the most recent line. The contract's
prefix-of-a-word fallback was the coordinator's assumption: out beta after
out alpha beta_suffix gives no shadow, nor echo beta nor beta. Measurement
refuted that rule; R-sh29-1 B chooses exact-prefix only.

Right, End, Ctrl-E and Ctrl-F accept the whole shadow. Alt-F accepts alpha
from alpha-beta/gamma; Ctrl-Right accepts alpha-beta/gamma, not the space.
Ctrl-R Enter accepts a selected line without executing. Escape restores
the draft. Fish Ctrl-C clears it; pelt deliberately restores instead.

Fish default shadow bytes are ESC[90m suffix ESC[39m on xterm-256color,
linux and COLORTERM=truecolor; no SGR 2. TERM=dumb draws no shadow. The
contract's indexed/RGB palette was wrong: the revised default is SGR 90,
16-colour-compatible on PAX; F2 may offer indexed/RGB PELT_COLORS choices.
At width 16 fish displays ghi plus an ellipsis after out abcdef, without
wrapping. No fish wrap bug was reproduced. Pelt reserves the final column
and truncates on cluster boundaries, avoiding a terminal pending-wrap state.

A unique file prefix ./uni suggests que-file plus a space. Multiple ./sha
matches suggest red-one plus a space, not just the common prefix red-.
The conservative pelt choice is a unique file suffix only; ambiguous
completion remains Tab's existing common-prefix/list UI. This departure
is a recommendation requiring the coordinator's ruling.

At this pin suggest and histsearch default off, independent of friendly_keys.
The argv[0] re-pin will turn both on with friendly per ruling #126. Leading
! remains shell input; no new expansion or command completion policy for it.
